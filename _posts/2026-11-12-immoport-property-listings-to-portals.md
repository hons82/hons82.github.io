Immoport helps real-estate agencies publish property listings to multiple portals without repeating the work by hand. It reads listings and photos from existing brokerage software, then prepares each export in the format that the target portal expects.

It supports OpenImmo, WIS-XML, FTP, FTPS and SFTP. Deliveries can run on a schedule or on demand, with logs and error reports to help keep listings in sync—including deletion notices when a property is taken offline.

Immoport runs in your own environment, so property data and portal credentials stay on your server. Learn more at [Immoport](https://web-immoport-40a004.gitlab.io/de/index.html).
